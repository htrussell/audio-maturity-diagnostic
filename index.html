    Audio Media Maturity Assessment

    A web-based digital maturity assessment tool calibrated to the audio media industry. This diagnostic instrument evaluates capabilities across data infrastructure, channels, operations, AI, and change capacity to provide actionable strategic profiles rather than generic score numbers.

    The Problem

    A digital maturity model is only as useful as its diagnostic layer. Generic frameworks applied to audio media (streaming, radio, podcasts) fail to surface specific capability gaps. For example, knowing a broadcaster's "data score" is low means nothing without diagnosing whether the issue is disjointed listener identifiers across systems or unmonitored streaming feeds.

    Our Methodology

    This tool operationalizes abstract capabilities into 15 observable, leveled diagnostic indicators. We established rigorous Stage 1 to Stage 5 definitions strictly calibrated against real-world audio media companies (from localized terrestrial radio to Spotify's AI experimentation platforms). The resulting scores compute a multidimensional radar map to classify the organization into one of five strategic archetypes.
